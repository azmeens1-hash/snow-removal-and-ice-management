Storm-night process

What happens on a storm night

From the first forecast to the final report, here's how we look after your property while you sleep.

  1. T-24 hrs

    Forecast Watch

    Our meteorologist flags the storm and schedules crews.

  2. T-6 hrs

    Brine Pre-Treat

    Lots and walks are treated to stop ice from bonding.

  3. 2″ Trigger

    Auto-Dispatch

    Plows roll out automatically, and you don't have to call.

  4. Every 2″

    Continuous Clearing

    Crews repeat passes until the storm has passed.

  5. Post-Storm

    Visit Report

    GPS times, photos and salt use are posted to your portal.
