Our story

From one pickup truck to 64 winter rigs

FrostGuard started in 2014 with one plow truck, one shovel crew and twelve driveways on Maple Ridge. Neighbours told neighbours, and the plazas and clinics followed.

Our promise hasn't changed since then. We show up before the storm, we clear it properly, and we prove every visit with photos. We still answer the phone ourselves at 3 AM.
